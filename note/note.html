Use case 09 · Dealer credit and receivables early warning

Dashboard: Dealer_Receivables_Watch.html (offline) · claude.ai/artifact/B4PadJtNYHR8RX53cXUydG · dummy data, descriptive only

Goal: show the health of receivables per dealer, explain what moves it, and point to the dealers to call first. No forecasting, machine learning or optimisation.

What each page answers

OverviewHow healthy are receivables, and who needs a call first? Key messages, six headline measures, ranked action list, overdue trend, overdue by region and aging bucket.
DriversWhat is moving overdue? Change by region and dealer, concentration, DSO split into days on terms and days paid late, roll rates, aging as % of receivables.
FlagsWho trips the rules, and do the rules work? New, continuing and cleared flags per month, 6-month back-test, top 15 dealers by limit use.
ExposureWhat could we lose? Collateral coverage, balance not covered per dealer, collateral by type, orders blocked, guarantees expiring within 90 days.
Dealers / Dealer detailSortable, searchable list of all dealers; click-through to one dealer's history, rules tripped, action, collateral and open invoices.
AppendixReading guide, glossary, scope, data and assumptions. First-time visitors get a 10-step guided tour; a ? icon explains each title and measure.

Filters on every page: as-of month, region, brand, tier, payment terms.

Main measures

Overdue shareOverdue ÷ receivables at month-end; overdue = past due date (invoice date + terms)
DSOReceivables ÷ last 3 months' sales × days
Days paid lateDSO minus days on terms (not-yet-due ÷ sales × days)
Roll rateShare of last month's bucket still unpaid this month (3-month average)
Limit useReceivables ÷ credit limit in force
Not coveredReceivables above collateral in force

Flags and thresholds

Overdue risingUp at each of the last 3 month-ends, by ≥ 5% each time, and ≥ ₺250k now
Limit over 90%Limit use > 90% (red above 100%)
90+ over 10%More than 10% of balance over 90 days past due
StatusAct = 2 or more flags · Watch = 1
Back-testOf dealers flagged 6 months earlier, share now 90+ over 10%

Brief: the first two flags. The third flag is an extension.

Assumptions

  • 140 dealers across Turkey's 7 regions, Arçelik or Beko, tier A/B/C, payment terms 60, 90 or 120 days; Nov 2024 to Oct 2026, in TRY. Invoices start May 2024 so opening balances are realistic.
  • Sales grow about 2.2% a month (inflation), with peaks in summer and November. Each dealer follows one payment pattern: on time, slow, improving, deteriorating from some month in 2026, or distressed.
  • Limits are reviewed every January and July, and frozen for dealers in trouble. Credit control cuts orders above 95% of the limit and almost stops them above 105%.
  • Guarantee letters run 12 months and are not renewed for dealers in trouble. The collections log reflects Oct 2026 only.
  • The back-test is flattered by dummy data; real hit rates will be lower. Rebuild everything with ./build.sh (fixed random seed).
